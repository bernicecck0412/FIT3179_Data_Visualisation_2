GDP Per Capita of Malaysia (2015-2024)

This dashboard visualise how Malaysia’s economic performance has evolved over the past decade.
GDP = Gross Domestic Product

GDP Per Capita of World Countries

GDP per Capita of Malaysia by State

Kuala Lumpur consistently recorded as highest GDP per capita. Other states such as Selangor and Penang also have relatively high GDP per capita compared to states like Kelantan and Perlis, which remain lighter in color.

This indicates that economic activities are more concentrated in urban and industrialized areas.

Ranking of States by GDP Per Capita

The ranking trend shows strong economic concentration in Malaysia’s capital and key urban regions. Kuala Lumpur and Labuan consistently dominate GDP per capita rankings, reflecting their financial and industrial strengths. Limited rank mobility among most states suggests persistent regional economic disparities and slower convergence across Malaysia’s state economies over time.

GDP of each State

Selangor has consistently recorded the highest GDP among all states. There was a noticeable drop in 2020, due to the COVID-19 pandemic, which affected most states' economic performance. However, the GDP gradually recovered in the following years. Kuala Lumpur also demonstrated steady increases over time.

By 2024, overall GDP of Malaysia reached its highest level within the ten-year period.

Population of each State

Selangor has the largest population, showing its role as Malaysia’s main economic and urban center. Most states have a balanced gender distribution, suggesting consistent demographic trends nationwide, while smaller states like Labuan and Perlis have much smaller populations, reflecting their limited size and economic activities.

GDP by Economic Activities

Malaysia's GDP shows strong Manufacturing dominance with steady growth across sectors. The COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 caused a temporary drop, but rapid recovery followed from June 2020 onward. Construction and Mining sectors demonstrate more volatility compared to stable manufacturing and Agriculture contributions.

Economic Activities by State

Most Malaysian states are service-based economies, with Services contributing over 50% to GDP in 13 out of 15 states/federal territories in 2024. Over 80% of Kuala Lumpur's economy comes from Services, while resource-rich Sabah and Sarawak show stronger Mining contributions. Manufacturing dominates in industrial hubs like Penang and Negeri Sembilan.
